SAMPLE EPISODE — TRANSCRIPT (fictional dialogue for demonstration)

INT. APARTMENT - NIGHT

SAM: Hey, what are you up to tonight?
ALEX: Nothing much. I was gonna stay in and catch up on some sleep, to be honest.
SAM: Come on, you can sleep when you're old. Let's hang out.
ALEX: Fine, but I'm not staying out late. I've got work in the morning.
SAM: Deal. First round's on me.

INT. BAR - LATER

SAM: So, how did the date go?
ALEX: Honestly? It was a total disaster.
ALEX: She spent the whole night on her phone, scrolling and laughing at something else.
SAM: Ouch. Did you at least split the bill?
ALEX: Nope. I picked up the tab, like an idiot.
SAM: Don't beat yourself up. It happens to everyone.
[Alex looks across the bar]
ALEX: Wait, is that who I think it is?
SAM: No way. I haven't seen her since college.
SAM: You should go say hi. What's the worst that could happen?
ALEX: She could pretend she doesn't remember me.
SAM: Just play it cool. Don't overthink it.

EXT. STREET - NIGHT

ALEX: I can't believe I ran into her after all these years.
SAM: And? Are you gonna ask her out or not?
ALEX: I don't know. I don't want to rush things.
SAM: Dude, you've been saying that for ten years.
ALEX: Okay, okay. I'll text her tomorrow.
SAM: Promise?
ALEX: I promise. Now can we please call it a night?
